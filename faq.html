About Pichani

What is Pichani?+

Pichani is event media management software. It gives organizers a branded online space for the photos, videos, and files created around an event.

Who is Pichani for?+

Pichani is for people organizing events, including festivals, conferences, and private celebrations, who want one place to manage media from their event community.

What media can I manage?+

Pichani is designed for event photos, videos, and files, which organizers can group into event collections.

How it works

How do I get started with an event?+

Create a dedicated event space and add the event context and collection structure your audience needs. You can then share the space before or during the event.

How do people add media?+

Share one link or QR code with photographers, partners, guests, and other contributors so they can add their own photos and videos.

Can I decide what appears in the gallery?+

Yes. Pichani lets organizers curate uploads and control what is visible before publishing a gallery or collection.

Sharing and sales

How can I share an event collection?+

Each event can have a permanent, shareable page, giving guests and stakeholders one destination for the event story.

Can I monetize event media?+

Yes. Depending on the event setup, organizers can offer access to a full album, contribution access, or individual photos and videos for purchase.

Can contributors earn from their work?+

When a buyer purchases a contributed media item, the contributor can earn from that sale.
