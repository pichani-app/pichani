COMMON QUESTIONS

A few useful details.

What is Pichani, and who is it for?+

Pichani is event-media software for organisers of festivals, conferences, private celebrations, and other shared experiences. It gives each event one branded place for photos, videos, and files.

Can guests and partners contribute media?+

Yes. Share a link or QR code with guests, photographers, partners, and other contributors, then curate what becomes part of the collection.

Can I control what is public?+

Yes. You can organise uploads into collections and choose what is visible before you publish a gallery.

What can I sell through Pichani?+

Depending on your event setup, you can offer access to a complete album or sell individual photos and videos. Contributors can earn when a media item they added is purchased.

Where can I learn more or get help?+
