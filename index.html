Frequently Asked Questions

What is mediaspace, and how does it work?+

Mediaspace is a software services that helps you get the most out of your events. It does this by helping you manage generated media content in ways that encourage sharing, fruitful interaction, and connection with your stakeholders. That includes enabling you to place shared media in the most potent place; your website. You and your audience (if you prefer) can add items to this space and can easily share them to social media

What are the benefits of using your SaaS product?+

(1) You have all your media in one place -- your website. (2) Easily sharable (3) Your stakeholders can easily contribute to it (4) Better connection with your stakeholders

How is mediaspace different from competitors+

You have your album on your website. Stakeholders such as people who attended your events can easily share them with links to your website appended. This helps drive traffic to your website.

How is my personal information stored and protected?+

We use advanced encryption methods to store your data securely. Access is restricted to authorized personnel, and we comply with applicable data protection laws

Do you share my data with third parties?+

We do not share your personal data with third parties without your consent, except when required to provide the service or comply with legal obligations

What measures do you have in place to protect my payment information?+

All payment transactions are processed through secure, PCI DSS-compliant gateways. Payment details are encrypted and never stored on our servers.

What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?+

When you cancel, your data is securely archived or deleted based on your request and applicable regulations. You can export your data at any time.

What is your pricing model?+

We offer tiered pricing plans based on usage, features, or number of users. Visit our pricing page for details.

Is there a free trial or demo available?+

Yes, we offer a 30-day free trial for any subscription you are intending to choose. You can also request a live demo with the team at any time

Are there any hidden fees?+

No, all costs are transparently listed in our pricing plan. Taxes or additional features may be extra, as outlined during checkout.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan anytime?+

Yes, plans are flexible. Changes can be made directly from your account

Can I monetize the media generated at my event?+

Yes. You can monetize your event by selling the media items generated in it.

What kinds of access and media can I monetize?+

You can charge for permission to view a full album, permission to contribute media to a space, and individual photos or videos inside an album. Contributors can also earn whenever an item they contributed is bought from your space.

Do you provide training or tutorials?+

Yes, we offer user guides, video tutorials, and live training sessions to ensure you and your team can use our product effectively.

What support options do you offer?+

We provide 24/7 customer support through chat, email, and phone. Our dedicated support team is always ready to assist. To get started, you can submit a ticket on the help page, or directly email us at help@pichani.com
